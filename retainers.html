Is this for you?

Let's be upfront about
who AYOS NA is built for.

A retainer only makes sense if it actually fits how you manage your property. Here's a quick, honest check.

This is for you if…

  • You own or manage a property in or near Betterliving / Parañaque
  • You're tired of finding a new technician every time something breaks
  • You want a documented history of what's been inspected, fixed or replaced
  • You manage the property remotely, or simply don't want to personally handle repairs
  • You'd rather have one accountable team than a rotating cast of contacts

This is not for you if…

  • Your property is outside our current Betterliving / Parañaque service area
  • You need guaranteed 24/7 emergency response, not priority scheduling
  • You're looking for a one-time cheap fix rather than an ongoing maintenance relationship
  • You already have a reliable in-house or go-to maintenance person you're happy with
  • You need major renovation or construction work rather than maintenance and repairs
